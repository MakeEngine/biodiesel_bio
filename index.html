What is Biodiesel?

Biodiesel is a biodegradable, renewable fuel manufactured from vegetable oils, animal fats, or recycled restaurant grease. Chemically, it consists of long-chain fatty acid methyl esters (FAME).

Unlike standard petroleum diesel which drills up ancient carbon from underground reservoirs, biodiesel recycles carbon dioxide that plants absorbed from the atmosphere during their growth cycle.

Myth vs. Scientific Reality

Common Myth

"Biodiesel destroys standard car engines."

Unmodified modern diesel engines can run cleanly on low-level blends (like B5 or B10 - 5% to 10% biodiesel). Higher blends (B20 or B100) may require minor fuel line material updates due to biodiesel's natural solvent properties.

Scientific Fact

"It has superior lubricity compared to petroleum."

Even small biodiesel blends drastically improve fuel lubricity, reducing wear and tear on high-pressure fuel injection pumps compared to ultra-low sulfur petroleum diesel.
